## Scope and omissions

2008: visually inspected PDF page 80 (printed 3-4), front matter PDF pages 1-3, general information 1-5, brake fluid 3-27 and clutch fluid 3-31. Front matter explicitly identifies E, F, ED and U area codes and CB1000R/RA. Exact market codes are offered for confirmation; unknown and general Europe are not silently mapped to an area code. The contents identifies the separate CB1000RA anti-lock brake chapter. No bleeding procedure or variant-specific numerical specification is copied. Follow the matching chapter before hydraulic work.

2008 spark replacement at 48,000 km is a separate individual choice because the displayed grid ends at 36,000 km. Inspection and replacement keys differ. The fluid grid and two-year note are both recorded. No anniversary, reset policy, later mileage table or automatic due logic is invented. Pre-ride work from the separate owner manual and the recommended dealer road test are reminders in the picker, not fabricated source task rows.

2023: visually inspected PDF pages 73-74 (printed 69-70). Evaporative emission inspection at 24,000 and 48,000 km is offered separately ONLY for confirmed ED, II ED, III ED and IV ED types. Common scheduled columns omit that conditional row and visibly explain the omission. Europe alone never proves type. Pre-ride-only fuel level, lights/horn and engine stop switch are not scheduled service cells; they remain part of the owner's separate pre-ride checks. Other pre-ride marks do not add duplicate scheduled tasks. Annual oil replacement is included; annual oil filter replacement is not. The 2021-22 workshop manual was not used. Valve clearance and all missing workshop values remain null; these templates provide no adjustment procedure.

Time additions start unchecked. Due status remains unknown because a reliable baseline, anniversary anchor and early-service reset are not defined here. Manual selection is required even when a job history exists. Repeated schedule choices use the actual printed columns; the application does not calculate future columns by modulo.

Structural validation checks data and references, not mechanical correctness. Independent content review must confirm every included and omitted row before changing local verification flags. Live release and the parked content-rights decision are separate. No new manual page or source render is hosted.
